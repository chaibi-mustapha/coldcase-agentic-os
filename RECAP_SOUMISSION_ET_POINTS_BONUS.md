# Building ColdCase AI: How Multi-Agent Systems & Google ADK Crack Decades-Old Criminal Mysteries

Unsolved criminal cases rarely remain cold due to a lack of evidence. More often, critical clues are fragmented across decades of sworn testimonies, hardwired bank and museum alarm telemetry, private investigator field reports, vintage newspaper microfilms, and internet sleuth archives.

To solve this challenge for the **Devpost All Things Agentic Hackathon**, I built **ColdCase AI** — an autonomous multi-agent criminal investigation operating system featuring a Hollywood-style interactive Crime Pinboard.

---

## 🧠 The Agentic Architecture: Powered by Google ADK & Gemini 3.7

Rather than relying on a single monolithic LLM prompt, ColdCase AI orchestrates a hierarchical pipeline of **6 specialized autonomous agents**:

1. **FBI & OSINT Archive Agent (Grade B2)**: Mines historical archives, declassified documents, and open intelligence databases.
2. **Timeline & Forensic Telemetry Agent (Grade A1)**: Reconstructs chronological checkpoints, sensor suppresses, and toll booth transponders.
3. **Alibi Contradiction Detector (Grade A1 Certified)**: Detects spatial-temporal impossibilities between sworn statements and physical evidence.
4. **Criminal Profiler & Hypothesis Agent (Grade B2/C3)**: Formulates testable theories (H1/H2) factoring in motives, insider access, and accomplice channels.
5. **Senior Detective Critic (Adversarial)**: Acts as a devil's advocate, stress-testing hypotheses using NATO STANAG source reliability matrices.
6. **Breakthrough Lead & Action Agent (High Impact)**: Recommends high-yield operational next steps (touch DNA, international rogatory letters, targeted subpoenas).

---

## 📌 The Cinematic Crime Board ("Murder Board")

To make multi-source intelligence intuitive for investigators, ColdCase AI renders all evidence on a dynamic, high-definition corkboard interface:
- **Authentic Polaroids & Pushpins**: With handwritten annotations and evidence grades.
- **Dynamic Catenary Red Strings**: Interactive SVG threads with drop-shadows connecting conflicting alibis and suspect sightings.
- **Multi-Source Clues**: Real historical clippings (*Berliner Zeitung, Boston Globe*), Private Investigator stakeout dossiers, and crowdsourced Reddit/Usenet leads.
- **2-in-1 Unified Splitters**: Smooth mouse-drag resizing and one-click collapsible panels.
