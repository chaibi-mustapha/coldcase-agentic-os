### 📖 3. About the Project (Markdown Story)

```markdown
## 💡 Inspiration
Decades-old unsolved crimes ("Cold Cases") remain open not for lack of clues, but because vital evidence is scattered across aging archives, uncorroborated depositions, and disconnected digital telemetries. Human investigators naturally suffer from cognitive fatigue and confirmation bias when navigating 20+ years of paper trails.

We built **ColdCase Agentic OS** to provide law enforcement with an autonomous, multi-agent AI system capable of cross-examining decades of testimonies, detecting mathematical alibi contradictions in seconds, and dynamically structuring crime relations on an interactive **"Murder Board"**.

## 🧠 What It Does
ColdCase Agentic OS deploys a coordinated pipeline of **6 specialized autonomous agents** built with **Google ADK & Gemini 3.5 Flash**:

1. **FBI & OSINT Archive Agent**: Fetches and downloads live intelligence dossiers and criminal photos from global open public archives in real time.
2. **Timeline & Forensics Agent**: Reconstructs minute-by-minute spatial checkpoints and alibi logs.
3. **Alibi Contradiction Agent**: Pairs conflicting depositions against digital toll telemetries, train manifests, and physical forensic proofs to expose mathematical impossibilities.
4. **Profiler & Hypothesis Agent**: Formulates high-probability theories with calibrated confidence scoring (0.0 to 1.0).
5. **Senior Detective Critic (Adversarial)**: Actively attacks the leading theory playing devil's advocate to eliminate investigative bias.
6. **Breakthrough Lead Agent**: Prescribes the single highest-yield operational next step (international warrants, touch DNA re-sequencing, targeted interrogation).

## 🖥️ Interactive Murder Board & Live Features
* **Interactive Murder Board Graph**: Complete Zoom & Pan navigation with force-directed visual clustering of suspects, alibis, and evidence.
* **Two-Tier Categorization & Live Search**: Instant filtering across Famous Heists, Homicides, Missing Persons, and Cyber / Crypto crimes.
* **Real Historical Cases**: Explores famous cases including the *Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum Heist*, *Zodiac 340 Cipher*, *D.B. Cooper*, and *Ruja Ignatova (OneCoin)*.
* **Real-time Open Intelligence Download**: Ingests real photos, summaries, and evidence from global open archives.

## ☁️ Google Cloud & Architecture
* **Google Cloud Run**: Serverless containerized deployment with scale-to-zero efficiency and low-latency global delivery.
* **Google ADK & Gemini 3.5 Flash**: Structured multi-agent workflow orchestration with typed Pydantic state persistence.
* **FastAPI Backend & Async Engine**: RESTful endpoints connecting live intelligence scrapers and graph generators.

## 🚀 What We Learned & Next Steps
Building adversarial multi-agent critique mechanisms showed us how AI can effectively prevent confirmation bias in high-stakes criminal investigations. Next, we plan to integrate multimodal video telemetry analysis and direct forensic DNA sequence matching.
```
